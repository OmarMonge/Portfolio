About

I'm a technical artist working at the seam between art and engineering — real-time rendering, procedural systems, and pipeline tooling. I like problems where a small amount of well-placed math produces a lot of visual variety.

Recent work centers on generative shaders: treating shader code as something a grammar can compose and an analyzer can reason about, rather than something written one expression at a time. I care about reproducibility, clean tooling, and demos people can actually poke at.
